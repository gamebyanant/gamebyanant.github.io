While playing any game, tap the bookmark icon to save it to your favorites. You can access all your saved games from the "Saved Games" section in your profile. This way, you can quickly replay the games you love most.
Your daily streak tracks how many consecutive days you've played Game Swipe. Open the app each day to increase your streak by 1. If you miss a day, your streak decreases by 1 instead of resetting completely — so don't worry about losing everything!
